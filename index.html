To My Dear Sara...

In the quiet of the morning, in the rush of the day, I find thoughts of you leading me astray. Across the labs and through the Swiss-bound dreams, Your brilliance is even brighter than it seems. You work with a fire that few could ever hold, A story of passion, waiting to be told. Yet even while chasing that high PhD peak, It’s your gentle kindness that makes my heart weak. I ask and long to carry you on my back so that I can have a 10 behind me like the GOAT, Messi I still remember how big a smile I had the night I got to my room after hanging out with you for the first time. I tho know, our time is limited here, tho shall I try making it much better than what you went through. The poem ends here, but my feelings do not, So answer me now, under Valentine's sky... Will you be my valentine? (I know the theme looks overused, but, only the theme is used more, not the poem!)
